All FAQs

What level is Cinder’s Cradle for?

The final region is tuned for characters approaching the new level cap of 50; the Ancient Gate also checks your Flame level. The prep checklist is in the zone guide.

Cinder’s Cradle guide

Is Sparkworks optional?

No. It sits in the 1.0 main loop, not beside it — treating it as a side system means grinding the old way at the new cap.

Sparkworks guide

How many spells did 1.0 add?

Nine new spells, plus eternal upgrades. Staff and summoner lines have the most immediate uses for them.

1.0 builds overview

Do Early Access saves carry over?

Yes. 1.0 moved saves to a new format; back up before migrating. What changes is your endgame plan, not your progress.
