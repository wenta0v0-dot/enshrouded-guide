Enshrouded 1.0 Builds & Classes — What Changed and Which Archetypes Gained

What 1.0 did to every build archetype: nine new spells, eternal upgrades, a cap of 50 — and the archetypes with the most to gain. Verified against the 1.0 notes.

10/17/2026 EnshGuide Last updated: 10/17/2026 3 min read

Quick Answer

Enshrouded 1.0 raised the level cap to 50, added nine new spells and eternal upgrades, and shook up every build archetype. This overview maps the archetypes players are running in 1.0 — Dagger, Summoner, Staff, Greatsword, Healer, Warrior and more — and which gained the most from the patch, verified against the 1.0 notes.

FAQ

What are the best builds in Enshrouded 1.0?
The 1.0 meta is still settling — the patch raised the cap to 50 and added nine new spells, so archetypes that scale with spells and eternal upgrades gained the most. This page tracks the verified archetype list and what each gained; deep per-build guides follow as numbers are confirmed in-game.
How many new spells did Enshrouded 1.0 add?
Nine new spells arrived with 1.0, alongside eternal upgrades. Spell-scaling archetypes (staff and summoner lines) have the most immediate uses for them.
Did 1.0 change the class system?
Enshrouded has no locked classes — builds come from the skill tree. 1.0 raised the cap to 50, which widens every tree and makes hybrid routes cheaper than they were in Early Access.
Where can I find a 1.0 tier list?
Right here as the meta settles. Beware older tier lists: several popular ones were last updated for Update 8, the final Early Access patch — they do not reflect 1.0.

Enshrouded has no locked classes — your build is your skill tree. What 1.0 did on October 15, 2026 was widen every tree (cap to 50), hand out nine new spells and eternal upgrades, and in the process reshuffle which archetypes are worth your points. This page maps the landscape honestly: what is verified, what is still settling, and which archetype pages are coming next.

State of the 1.0 meta

The patch is days old and the meta is still settling — any site handing you a finished “S-tier” list right now is guessing. What we can already verify: which archetypes the patch touched, which new spells slot where, and which old tier lists are out of date. Rankings harden here as numbers get confirmed in-game.

The archetypes players are running in 1.0

The build community moved fast — the established guide sites published 1.0 rewrites within 48 hours of launch. The archetypes with 1.0-specific coverage so far:

All of the above are verified build archetypes with post-1.0 coverage in the community. Full write-ups per archetype — skill nodes, eternal upgrade picks, gear targets — are being verified in-game and land here one by one.

What 1.0 changed for every build

Three patch-wide changes that touch all archetypes, verified against the 1.0 notes:

  • Cap 40 → 50. Ten more levels is ten more nodes: every archetype got wider, and hybrid routes that were too expensive in Early Access got cheaper.
  • Nine new spells. The spell pool grew around caster and summoner lines first — if your build scales with spells, 1.0 was your patch.
  • Eternal upgrades. The eternal system expanded alongside the cap; it is the long-term sink for every archetype.

Beware pre-1.0 tier lists

Several popular build pages were last touched for Update 8 (“Forging the Path”) — the final Early Access patch, two days before 1.0. They are not wrong about Update 8; they are wrong about now. Check the page’s date before you spend points on its advice.
