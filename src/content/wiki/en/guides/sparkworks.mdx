**Sparkworks** is the new system Enshrouded 1.0 shipped with on **October 15, 2026** — introduced alongside the final region [Cinder's Cradle](/guides/cinders-cradle/) and the raised level cap of 50, and tied to the game's new endgame progression. Days after launch, the official community wiki still had **no hub page for it**. This page is that page: what is verified so far, and the mechanics being confirmed in-game right now.

<Callout type="info" title="The honest status">
  Sparkworks is new enough that most coverage treats it as a footnote. What is verified today: **it is a 1.0 system, it sits in the main progression loop, and it connects to the endgame route.** The detailed mechanics — costs, outputs, optimal order — are being **verified in-game** and land on this page as they are confirmed. We would rather ship the verified half than invent the rest.
</Callout>

## Where Sparkworks fits in the 1.0 loop

The 1.0 release restructured the end of the game: the cap moved to 50, the final region opened, and Sparkworks arrived as part of that progression — not as a side minigame. The practical reading:

1. **It is part of the main loop.** Plan your days around it the way you plan flame levels and skill points — skipping it means grinding the pre-1.0 way at the new cap.
2. **It connects to the endgame route.** Cinder's Cradle is tuned for characters who engaged with the 1.0 systems; Sparkworks is one of them.
3. **It is undocumented on the big wikis.** Which is exactly why this page exists — and why every claim here carries a date.

## How Sparkworks works

Verified against Keen Games' official announcement and the community mechanics guide at [enshrouded.world](https://www.enshrouded.world/wiki/sparkworks) (October 2026):

- **It is a logic system for your builds** — the official pitch is "turn your build into an adventure": you connect triggers to mechanisms and the world responds.
- **Inputs**: levers, buttons, and trigger zones.
- **Outputs**: doors, traps, and spawned events.
- **Switchboxes** connect inputs to outputs, wired with the **Sparkworks Tool**.
- **Logic operators** include clocks and logic gates — the building blocks for anything from a timed door to a trap sequence.

## How to start building with it

The community guide's workflow, which matches how every logic system is learned:

1. **Start simple** — one lever, one door. Get the connection working before anything clever.
2. **Wire input to output** with the Switchbox and the Sparkworks Tool.
3. **Add conditions and delays only after** the basic connection works.
4. **Test both behaviors**: does it trigger when it should, and does it *reset* when it should? A door that opens once is not the same build as a door that works repeatedly.
5. Finished creations can be shared through **Adventure Sharing** (the system introduced with Update 8).
