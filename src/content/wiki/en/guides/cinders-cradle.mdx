## What Cinder's Cradle is

Verified against the official 1.0 changelog:

- **The final region of the map.** Every zone before it was a prologue to this one — it is where the level-50 route ends.
- **A volcanic endgame zone.** New enemies tuned for the raised cap, with new spells and gear as the rewards.
- **Opened on October 15, 2026** by the 1.0 release, alongside the Sparkworks system and nine new spells.

## How to prepare for it

The zone is tuned for the new cap, so preparation is mostly about arriving complete:

1. **Level toward 50.** The new cap is not cosmetic — Cinder's Cradle's enemies assume you have spent the wider tree.
2. **Pick up the new spells.** Nine arrived with 1.0; several change how the endgame's encounters play. See the [1.0 builds overview](/guides/tier-list/).
3. **Rebuild Early Access habits.** The building system changed and the save format moved — guides from before October 15 describe a different game.
4. **Stock up before the descent.** The volcanic endgame punishes under-supplied runs; consumables and backup gear are cheaper than a lost expedition.
