Enshrouded has no locked classes — your build is your skill tree. What 1.0 did on October 15, 2026 was widen every tree (**cap to 50**), hand out **nine new spells** and **eternal upgrades**, and in the process reshuffle which archetypes are worth your points. This page maps the landscape honestly: what is verified, what is still settling, and which archetype pages are coming next.

## What 1.0 changed for every build

Three patch-wide changes that touch all archetypes, verified against the 1.0 notes:

- **Cap 40 → 50.** Ten more levels is ten more nodes: every archetype got wider, and hybrid routes that were too expensive in Early Access got cheaper.
- **Nine new spells.** The spell pool grew around caster and summoner lines first — if your build scales with spells, 1.0 was your patch.
- **Eternal upgrades.** The eternal system expanded alongside the cap; it is the long-term sink for every archetype.

## Beware pre-1.0 tier lists

Several popular build pages were last touched for **Update 8 ("Forging the Path")** — the final Early Access patch, two days before 1.0. They are not wrong about Update 8; they are wrong about now. Check the page's date before you spend points on its advice.
