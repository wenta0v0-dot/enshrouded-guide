Enshrouded 1.0 is out (October 15, 2026), and it changes where the game ends: the final region **Cinder's Cradle** is open, the **level cap is 50**, and a new system called **Sparkworks** sits on top of the old progression. Whether you are new or returning from Early Access, this is the route that gets you there without wasting days.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  1.0 route in three moves: **(1)** run the established zone progression to rebuild your power under the new cap, **(2)** put points into the **nine new spells** and eternal upgrades as they unlock, **(3)** treat **Sparkworks** as part of your main loop, not a side system — then head into **Cinder's Cradle**.
</Callout>

## What 1.0 actually changed

Verified against the official release changelog (October 15, 2026):

- **Cinder's Cradle is open** — the final area of the map, a volcanic endgame region with new enemies tuned for the raised cap.
- **Level cap: 50** — with new spells and gear to earn along the way. Nine new spells arrived with 1.0 alone, plus eternal upgrades.
- **Sparkworks** — a new progression system with its own loop. It is new enough that the official community wiki's hub page for it was still missing days after launch; our [Sparkworks guide](/guides/sparkworks/) tracks what it does and how it fits.
- **Building overhauled** — the building system got major changes; base plans from Early Access guides need re-checking.
- **New save format** — Early Access saves carry over, but back up before you migrate.

## The post-1.0 route, in order

1. **Returning player: migrate, then re-plan.** Your EA base and character carry into the new save format. Your old endgame plan does not — the cap moved to 50 and the final zone moved under your feet.
2. **New player: run the zones in order.** The early and mid zones are unchanged in shape; they are your on-ramp to the new level cap. Follow the skill tree toward one damage style first, spread later.
3. **Spend early points on a core loop.** One movement pattern, one damage pattern, one survival pattern. The respec-friendly skill tree forgives, but a focused first build reaches Cinder's Cradle sooner.
4. **Pick up the new spells as they appear.** Nine new spells shipped with 1.0 — several slot into existing build archetypes immediately. The [1.0 builds overview](/guides/tier-list/) maps which archetypes gained the most.
5. **Learn Sparkworks as part of the loop.** It is new enough that most Early Access habits skip it. Our [Sparkworks guide](/guides/sparkworks/) covers what it does and when it matters.
6. **Prepare, then enter Cinder's Cradle.** The endgame region is tuned for the new cap. The [Cinder's Cradle guide](/guides/cinders-cradle/) has the preparation checklist.

## Three mistakes post-1.0 players make

1. **Following an Early Access guide to the letter.** Building, saves and the cap all changed on October 15 — any guide that still says "Early Access" is describing a different game.
2. **Skipping Sparkworks.** It is new, it is undocumented on the big wikis, and it sits in the main progression loop — skipping it means grinding the old way at the new cap.
3. **Entering Cinder's Cradle underleveled.** It is the final region, tuned for the new cap. Prep first; the zone does not forgive.
