[Enshrouded YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Enshroudedgame). They are
cropped locally to remove the trailer's "ALL FOOTAGE CAPTURED IN-GAME." caption,
lightly graded, and re-encoded for the web. Game imagery remains © Keen Games
GmbH; the site is an unofficial fan guide and credits the trailer on-page.
